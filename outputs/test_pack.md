# Sample test pack

Generated by `python -m scripts.make_test_pack`. Every decision here came from running the system, not from hand-writing an example.

**Inputs.** The eight policies in `data/sample_policies/` and the 28 labelled claims in `data/test_claims.json`.

**Result.** 26 of 28 match their label (93%) using the keyword baseline reader, which needs no API key. Every claim's full decision, including the findings and their citations, is in `outputs/assessments.json`.

A worked example of each outcome follows.

## Where it disagrees with the labels

Kept in, not hidden. These are the two cases the write-up discusses: the baseline cannot follow a cross-reference, and it matched a windshield to a body-panel depreciation rate.
